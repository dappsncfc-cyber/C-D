Meet The Team

"A dedicated family of professionals providing compassionate and sympathetic care to North Norfolk for decades."

Adrian Amis

Owner, Funeral Arranger & Director

Joining the business in 2002, Adrian is the owner and manager. He is dedicated to carrying on the family business and continuing to be Cromer’s only Independent Family Funeral Directors. In 2025 Adrian acquired Goward's Independent Funeral Services, Fakenham's only Independent Family Funeral Directors.

Catherine

Funeral Arranger

After two decades in hospitality and wedding management, Catherine transitioned into funeral arranging—a move that has felt like finding her true calling. It is a privilege to support families during their most difficult moments, ensuring every detail is handled with the utmost dignity and care.

David

Bearer and Driver

Joined the business in 2019 after over 30 years working in the NHS. David brings a wealth of experience in patient care and transport, ensuring a respectful and smooth service for every family we care for.

Barrie

Bearer and Driver

Started in October 2020 after previously working for Dignity and the CO-OP. Barrie's extensive background in the funeral industry makes him a vital part of our professional operations.

Michael

Bearer and Driver

After 20 years working in public service, Michael has been in the funeral industry now for over 10 years. His dedication to public service translates into the exceptional care he provides to families.

Peter

Bearer and Driver

Joined our staff when we took over Goward's. Formerly worked at Crisp Maltings, Peter brings a local understanding and dedicated work ethic to the Fakenham and North Norfolk community.

John

Bearer and Driver

Previously worked for many years at GlaxoSmithKline. John's attention to detail and professional demeanour ensure that families receive a service that is both dignified and highly efficient.

Kieran

Bearer

Adrian's brother, Kieran assists the team when he isn't working his primary role as a workshop controller or spending time with his family.
