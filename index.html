About Us

Cromer and District Independent Funeral Services is a family-run business deeply rooted in the heart of Norfolk's charming coastal town, Cromer. With a compassionate and personal approach, we offer a comprehensive range of funeral services tailored to the unique needs of each family.

Our Heritage: A Legacy of Care in North Norfolk

At Cromer & District Independent Funeral Services, we believe that being more than just a business is what defines us. For over three decades, our family-run independent funeral home has stood as a pillar of support for those navigating the difficult days following a loss. Founded by Leslie and Jean, our firm was built on a foundation of compassionate funeral services and a deep-rooted commitment to the people of Cromer, Sheringham, and the surrounding coastal villages.

A Journey Rooted in Tradition

The story of our growth is one of personal dedication. In 2002, Adrian joined the family firm, following the path set by his grandfather, Jimmy Mallett. Starting from the ground up as a funeral bearer and grave digger, Adrian gained an intimate understanding of every aspect of professional funeral arrangements. This hands-on experience proved invaluable when he took the helm in 2013, ensuring the legacy of Leslie and Jean continued even through the personal heartbreak of Jean’s passing in 2018.

Today as the sole owner and proprietor Adrian’s leadership has evolved the company to meet the changing needs of our community and clients, including specialised work for H.M. Coroners, while never losing sight of the traditional funeral cortège and local customs that our neighbours value.

Expanding Our Compassionate Reach

As an independent firm, we answer to the families we serve, not to corporate shareholders, we strive to provided one to one care to all our customers and create the funeral experience they require. This independence allowed Adrian to expand our vision in 2025 by incorporating Gowards Funeral Services in Fakenham. By bringing another respected independent family firm into our fold, we have extended our ability to offer personalised funeral care across a wider area of North Norfolk, from the historic streets of Holt to the quiet reaches of Mundesley and Overstrand.

Whether you are enquiring about how to register a death, seeking a dignified direct cremation, or visiting a loved one in our private Chapel of Rest at West Street, you will find the same personal touch that has been our cornerstone since 1987. With modern facilities and a dedicated team, we are prepared to provide the best service on demand, while always looking toward the future and the next generation of family care.

What to do when someone dies

"With our help and guidance, we will endeavour to make it as straightforward as possible."

Sometimes a death may be expected, but nothing prepares us for the emotional shock of losing someone close. Making funeral arrangements can seem very confusing but with our help and guidance, we will endeavour to make it as straightforward as possible.

Changes From the 9th September 2024

Following a death and after being notified, the doctor will complete the death certificate (MCCD) and then email it to a Medical Examiner along with the medical records. The Medical Examiner looks at all the information and contacts the next of kin/family to confirm that the information is correct. Once satisfied, the Medical Examiner will then email the certificate to the registrars allowing the next of kin/family to make an appointment to register the death.

When someone dies at home

Please contact your own Doctor or on call Doctor, district nurse or terminal care nurse, if you cannot reach your doctor call 111. When one of these medical professionals has confirmed the death, you can then contact ourselves. It does not matter what the time of day it is, we are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days of the year.

There is no rush and if you wish to spend more time with your loved one at home you are welcome to do so. When you are ready, two members of our staff will then bring your loved one into our care.

When someone dies in hospital

You no longer need to attend the hospital to collect the medical cause of death certificate, this will be sent electronically from the hospital to the Medical Examiner. The Medical Examiner will contact you.

When someone dies in a nursing home

The staff may be qualified to confirm that death has taken place or alternatively the home will arrange for a doctor to visit. Once the death has been confirmed, the home or yourselves can contact us to make arrangements for bringing your loved one into our care, at any time day or night. The doctor will send the medical cause of death to the Medical Examiner and the Medical Examiner will contact you.

When someone dies unexpectedly

If a doctor is unable to confirm the cause of death, if it is unexpected or an accident, the coroner may be involved. The coroner will arrange for your loved one to be taken to a local hospital, this will be carried out by a funeral director who is contracted by the coroner (you do not have to use this funeral director for the funeral arrangements). The coroner and ourselves will keep you informed as to what to do next depending on the situation.

Registration Procedure

A member of the family should ideally register the death. If this is not possible it should be someone who was present at the death, the person organising the funeral or the occupier of the premises in which the death occurred.

An appointment can only be booked once you have been contacted by either the Medical Examiner or the Coroners office; they will advise you that you can now make an appointment and this must be completed within 5 days of this authorisation.

Legal Requirement: A death must be registered by law, in the district or sub-district in which it has taken place. The Registrar will be unable to talk to or see anyone without a prior appointment.

Deaths involving the Coroner

If reported to the Coroner, the Registrar cannot register the death until enquiries are complete. You will be advised when registration can take place. If an inquest is held, you will not need to attend an appointment to register the death.

Making Arrangements

Funeral arrangements can be made at our office or if it is more convenient, in the comfort of your own home. At our initial meeting we will explain the many options available and ask a number of questions. It is probable that you will not know all the answers and rather than rush into making the wrong decision, it is often best to take your time and to discuss it further with your wider family.

Regardless of beliefs or culture we are able to offer you many different ways to make the funeral an individual, special and memorable experience. We can arrange a traditional funeral or an alternative funeral that suits your needs.

Funeral Plans

A funeral plan is an easy way to arrange the funeral you want in advance. A plan allows you to specify your wishes and pay for the funeral director’s services included in your plan. As customers often tell us, that can bring peace of mind to you and your family.

A plan from Golden Charter also includes an allowance towards third party costs. These are essential, non-funeral director services such as the cremation or burial fees, plus the minister or officiant’s fee to perform the service. The value of the allowance included in your plan may grow over time. There will only be more to pay at the time of the funeral if the third party costs exceed the allowance when you buy your plan, or if the costs increase by more than the growth of your plan.

At Cromer and District Independent Funeral Services LTD, we work in partnership with Golden Charter to provide you with a simple way to plan ahead. As one of the UK’s largest funeral plan providers1, Golden Charter has helped over 1 million people to plan ahead2, so we’re completely confident in offering their plans to you.

Everyone has different needs when it comes to funeral planning, so we make sure the plans we offer are as flexible as possible. When you get in touch, we’ll be happy to discuss your funeral requirements and tailor a plan to suit you. Rest assured a plan from Golden Charter is available to all UK residents, regardless of age or state of health.

Would you like to know more information about how a Golden Charter funeral plan could work for you? We can provide you with all of the information you need to make an informed decision for you and your family, so get in touch today!
