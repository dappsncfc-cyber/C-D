Direct Unattended Cremation

Price from £1350.00 including disbursements*
  • The provision of the Funeral Director's services and attending to all necessary arrangements for the direct unattended cremation without guests permitted at Cromer Crematorium, who would choose the date and time of the direct unattended cremation.
  • Removal of your loved one into our Chapel of Rest.
  • A Simple Coffin with a name plate.
  • Provision of the Private Ambulance and Funeral Director from our Chapel of Rest at 32 West Street to Cromer Crematorium.
  • Inform the next of kin once the ashes are ready to collect.
  • Additional Services Available - Dressing £50.00, Viewing £50.00.

Direct Unattended Burial

£1750.00 including our fees and disbursements *

A burial without Ceremony, known as an unattended burial, is for those, who for various reasons, prefer not to have a funeral service. This option of a simple, straightforward burial is now being chosen by a growing number of people.

Services included in this option:

  • All our Professional Services.
  • Removal of your loved one to our Chapel of Rest.
  • A Simple Coffin with name plate.
  • Grave Marker Cross.
  • Grave Digging Fee.
  • Suitable vehicle to travel to Churchyard/Cemetery.

*Please note this does not include Burial Fee which range typically from £399.00 (Church of England fee for Churchyards).

Service of Remembrance

£4300.00* including disbursements

A Service of Remembrance for Military Veterans

A Remembrance funeral is a fitting way to pay tribute to the brave men and women who have served in the armed forces. Providing a dignified farewell and remembering those who have served and defended their country. Providing an opportunity to honour their service, sacrifices and pay our respects.

This package includes all the provisions of our Standard Funeral as well as our Union Jack flag to shroud the coffin, Provision of a Poppy wreath and a 'Poppy Fields' scatter tube (as above image).

A bugle player to perform The Last Post (subject to availability).

*A standard-bearer can be arranged (subject to availability).

Disbursement Fees

Each funeral will incur fees from third parties, these include:

  • CREMATIONS - Crematorium Fee, Minister, Humanist or Civil Celebrant Fee.
  • BURIALS - Church Fee (including grave digger and grave marker, and heating in winter months), Verger Fee and the Organist's Fee.

All costs vary depending on the chosen Crematorium or Church. We would confirm all prices directly with you as and when required.

Additional Fees

Other additional costs to consider include:

  • Obituary in the Local Newspaper
  • Order of Service Booklets
  • Purchase of Exclusive Rights for a Burial Plot
  • Interment and Admin Fees
  • Floral Arrangements
  • Additional Transport
  • Webcast or Videography
  • Ashes Casket or Scatter Tube
