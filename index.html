Practical Guidance & Bereavement Articles

Help and Support

Sometimes a death may be expected, but nothing prepares us for the emotional shock of losing someone close. Making funeral arrangements can seem very confusing, but with our help and guidance, we will endeavour to make it as straightforward as possible.

What To Do When Someone Dies

1. Medical Certificate

If a death occurs at home or in a care home, contact the GP or call NHS 111. A Medical Examiner / Doctor will issue the Medical Certificate of Cause of Death (MCCD) directly to the Registrar. Call us at any hour on 01263 514814 to bring your loved one into our Chapel of Rest.

2. Registering the Death

Once the Medical Examiner has contacted you, book an appointment at a Norfolk Registration Office (such as Cromer, North Walsham, Holt, or Norwich) to register the death within 5 days and obtain the green form for burial or cremation.

3. Arranging the Funeral

Contact Adrian and the team at 32 West Street. We will handle all forms, coordinate with the crematorium, church, cemetery, and celebrant, and guide you at your own pace.
